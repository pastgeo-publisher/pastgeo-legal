Privacy Policy — PastGeo Publisher

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. What Data This App Collects

PastGeo Publisher is an internal tool used solely by the owner of the TikTok account @pastgeo. The app accesses only the minimum data needed to publish videos to that account via TikTok's Content Posting API:

  • Account authorization tokens (to authenticate API requests)
  • Video files, captions, and cover images the account owner provides for publishing

2. What Data This App Does NOT Collect

This app does not collect, store, or share:

  • Data from any TikTok user other than the @pastgeo account owner
  • Personal information of viewers, followers, or commenters
  • Location data, device data, or browsing history
  • Any data for advertising or analytics purposes

3. How Data Is Used

Authorization tokens are used only to authenticate API requests to publish content to the @pastgeo account. Video files and captions are transmitted to TikTok solely for the purpose of publishing them to that account.

4. Data Storage

Authorization tokens are stored securely and are never shared with third parties. Published videos remain subject to TikTok's own data practices.

5. Third Parties

This app does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to the @pastgeo account owner via TikTok direct message.
